Meal planning for babies, kids and the whole household

Your plan, in their language.

NannyPal is a meal planner for families and their nanny, maid or domestic helper. You plan the week. Your helper gets every recipe rewritten in simple words, step by step, in English and the language they read best.

No spam. One email at launch.

Illustration only. Final app screens may look different.

How it works

Plan once. Everyone knows what's for dinner.

  1. You plan the week

    Fill it in yourself or let the app suggest meals. Allergens are skipped automatically.

  2. Your helper gets it in their language

    Simple steps, in English plus the language they chose. The shopping list follows the plan.

  3. Dinner comes out the way you pictured it

    Fewer "what did you mean?" messages.

What makes it different

Recipes your helper can actually follow

Most recipes assume you already know how to cook the dish, and all the fancy culinary lingo that comes with it. NannyPal rewrites them in plain, simple language first, then puts them in your helper's language.

Before

Sauté the diced onion until translucent, then deglaze with a splash of stock and reduce.

After, in simple words

1. Cook the onion in oil for 5 minutes.
2. Add a little stock.
3. Stir until it is half as much.

An example of the kind of rewriting the app does. Wording in the final app may differ.

Who it's for

Babies and kids first. Adults too.

NannyPal starts with the smallest people in your house: what they eat, when they eat it and what they can't have. The same plan then covers everyone else at the table.

Baby and toddler guidance is based on NHS advice. AI can get things wrong, so please double-check allergens and ingredients. More below.

  • Family allergens saved once and skipped in suggestions
  • Meal slots you set yourself, snacks included
  • Adults' meals and family dinners in the same weekly plan
  • One shopping list for the household, which you can assign

Two modes, one plan

Built for the person cooking and the person planning

Your view

Everything in one place: you're in charge of the plan, recipes, allergens and delegating the shopping.

Their view

A simpler version with just what's needed: today's meals, simplified step-by-step recipes and the shopping list, without clutter.
