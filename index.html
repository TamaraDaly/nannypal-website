How it works

Plan once. Everyone knows what's for dinner.

  1. You plan the week

    Fill the week's meals yourself or let the app suggest them. Suggestions skip your family's allergens and avoid repeating the same main meal.

  2. Your helper gets it in their language

    Each recipe is rewritten in short, simple steps, then shown in English plus the language your helper chose. The shopping list follows the same plan.

  3. Dinner comes out the way you pictured it

    Fewer "what did you mean?" messages, fewer wrong groceries and less cooking from memory.

What makes it different

Recipes your helper can actually follow

Most recipes assume you already know how to cook the dish. NannyPal rewrites them in plain, simple language first, then puts them in your helper's language.

Before

Sauté the diced onion until translucent, then deglaze with a splash of stock and reduce.

After, in simple words

1. Cook the onion in oil for 5 minutes.
2. Add a little stock.
3. Stir until it is half as much.

Who it's for

Babies and kids first. Adults too.

NannyPal starts with the smallest people in your house: what they eat, when they eat it and what they can't have. The same plan then covers everyone else at the table.

  • Family allergens saved once and avoided in automatic suggestions
  • Meal slots you set yourself, including snacks
  • Adults' meals and family dinners sit in the same weekly plan
  • One shopping list for the whole household

Two modes, one plan

Built for the person cooking and the person planning

Purple is you. Teal is your helper. The colours stay the same across the app, so nobody edits the wrong thing.

Parent mode

Everything you need to run the kitchen calendar.

  • Weekly plan with breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
  • Recipe cookbook that belongs to your household
  • Shopping list built from the plan
  • Your family's allergens and meal times in one place

Helper mode

A simpler view with only what's needed to cook and shop.

  • Today's meals up front
  • Step-by-step recipes with photos
  • Everything in English plus one chosen language
  • Like or dislike a dish, and send a question or note back to you

Your own cookbook

Bring in the recipes you already love

Found a dish on a website or saved a caption you liked? Add it to your household cookbook and NannyPal rewrites it into short, clear steps your helper can follow.

  • Import from a web link, or paste a caption or screenshot
  • Instructions are rewritten in simple steps, not copied word for word
  • The original creator is credited and linked
  • Recipes are for your household only and can't be shared or sold from the app
